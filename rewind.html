How the recap is calculated

Statistics use the best eligible tournament-point attempt per player and song, following the scoreboard’s rules. Points = level × 100 × grade factor. Machine scores do not determine tournament points.

Perfect share is Perfect judgments divided by all five judgments on complete records. It is not the game’s accuracy rating. Unknown values are excluded, never treated as zero.

All standings are final for the completed tournament. Regenerate reloads the latest published results; previously downloaded images remain snapshots.
